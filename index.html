The tip truth

The three digits are the whole manual: the first, doubled, is the fan width in inches; the last two are the orifice in thousandths. A 515 throws a 10-inch fan from an orifice of 15 thousandths - a tip that sprays latex, not stain.

The coverage truth

The can says 350 square feet a gallon, and the sprayer says some of every gallon lands on the drop cloth. With the overspray allowance priced in, the purchase either covers the coats or runs dry mid-wall.

The pace truth

Flow rate times honest coverage is the trigger-minutes the job wants. The sprayer is fast - but only as fast as the number with the loss priced in, and the estimate should say so.

What it never does

It never reads the can at face value, never bills the drop cloth's quarter to the wall, and never forgets the norms are labeled - the drop cloth keeps the honest ledger.

Spray Math is open source - the engine is small enough to read, and the math is plain enough to argue with.
